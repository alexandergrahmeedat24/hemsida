🔥 Fire Guide (DayZ)

Making a fire is essential for survival. Follow these steps:

  • 1. Gather materials: Sticks + bark or rags.
  • 2. Craft a fireplace kit: Combine sticks with bark.
  • 3. Place the fireplace: Put it on the ground.
  • 4. Add fuel: Add more sticks or firewood.
  • 5. Start the fire: Use matches, a lighter, or a hand drill kit.
Tip: Always cook meat before eating and avoid getting wet to prevent sickness.

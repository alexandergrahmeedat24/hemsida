Survival Tips

These practical tips help you stay alive longer and avoid common mistakes in DayZ.

Tips & Tricks

  • Move quietly and avoid large groups of zombies.
  • Always carry a secondary weapon and extra ammo.
  • Use terrain and buildings to hide from other players.
  • Keep blood and health levels stable by eating and treating wounds.
  • Mark your base location on the map and avoid obvious positions.

Quick survival rules

  1. Check your surroundings before looting.
  2. Stay warm when it rains or snows.
  3. Keep hydrated and avoid dirty water.

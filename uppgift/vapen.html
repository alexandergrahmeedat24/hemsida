Weapons and Combat

Weapons in DayZ are vital for self-defense and resource gathering. Learn where to find them and how to use them safely.

Top weapon choices

  • Rifles are best for long-range engagement.
  • Shotguns handle close-range threats well.
  • Pistols are useful as backup weapons.
  • Melee weapons are noisy but reliable in stealth situations.

How to find weapons

Military bases and medical tents are the best spots for guns and ammunition. Avoid open fields and always approach carefully.

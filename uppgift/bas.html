Base Building Guide

A base in DayZ is your safest way to store loot and survive long-term.

1. Choosing the Right Location

Stay away from big cities and military zones. Good base spots include forests, hidden barns, or remote villages. The less visible your base is, the longer it survives.

2. Tools You Need

  • Axe or Hatchet
  • Saw
  • Shovel or Pickaxe
  • Pliers
  • Hammer or Hatchet

3. Materials

  • Wooden logs
  • Wooden planks
  • Nails
  • Metal wire
  • Combination lock

4. Building Your First Fence

Craft a fence kit using sticks and rope. Place it, then use a shovel to set it up. Add logs first, then planks, and secure everything with nails.

5. Making a Gate

Use metal wire and pliers to turn your fence into a gate. Add a combination lock so other players can’t get in.

6. Storage

Use crates, barrels, or tents to store your loot. Always keep your most valuable items on you when logging out.

7. Defense Tips

  • Use multiple walls
  • Hide your base, don’t build in the open
  • Log out inside your base
  • Check for raids often

Food and Hydration

Here is how to find food in DayZ and keep your character alive.

How to find food

  • Search farmhouses, fishing villages and hunting cottages for canned food, bread and dairy.
  • Look inside barns, sheds and caravans for fresh vegetables and fruit.
  • Pick berries and mushrooms from bushes and forests, but avoid poisonous ones.
  • Hunt animals like chickens, deer and boar for meat and cook it before eating.
  • Loot supermarkets, gas stations and trailers for snacks, energy bars and water.

Food locations

Good spots are farms, small villages, coastal areas and remote houses. These places often contain food boxes, fridges, and cooking supplies.

Stay safe while looting

  • Move quietly and check for zombies before entering buildings.
  • Keep your backpack light so you can run if needed.
  • Bring a knife or axe for harvesting vegetables and skinning animals.
